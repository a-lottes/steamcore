## 6. Verdict

**Passed — and this closes the feature.** F9 is genuinely fixed, and I checked the code rather than the claim: `readSignal()` still returns early for the three buttons, then samples only when `signal == InputSignal::kUp` — resetting `cachedX_`/`cachedY_` to `kNeutral` *first*, then issuing exactly one `adc_oneshot_read` per channel and updating `lastRawX_`/`lastRawY_` beside the cached levels from those two fresh samples (`analog_joystick_source.cpp:119-134`) — while `kDown`/`kLeft`/`kRight` touch no hardware at all and fall through to `directionActive(signal, cachedX_, cachedY_)` (`:135`). That is 2 reads per tick counted from the code, down from F4's 4 and the original 8, and it closes both consequences F4 left narrowed: one sample pair now produces all four booleans and both logged raws, so `up=`/`left=` in the harness line are finally beside the samples that actually produced them (NFR-5, now ✅), and left-and-right-true-in-one-tick has stopped being representable at all rather than merely improbable — the impossible state Decision 2 exists to prevent is now unreachable by construction, not by luck. The reset-before-read ordering is the detail that makes the failure path still correct: an axis whose read fails falls back to `kNeutral` for that tick instead of holding the previous tick's deflection.
